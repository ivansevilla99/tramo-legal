Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Tramo has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your trips, profile and stats are stored only on your iPhone or Android phone. Tramo Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple and RevenueCat (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). We never sell or share your data for advertising.

Tramo for Android

The Android version works the same in essentials: no accounts, no ads and no tracking. What differs from the iPhone version:

  • Your data (trips, history, level, profile and preferences) is stored only on your phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings.
  • Map, routes and place search are provided by Google (Google Maps Platform: Maps, Routes and Places). To compute a route or search for a place, Tramo sends Google the start and destination points or the text you type, without your name or any identifier of yours. Google can see the IP address of the request and handles data under its privacy policy.
  • Weather comes from Open-Meteo (the area you are in is sent, without identifying you).
  • Tramo Pro purchases are handled by Google Play. Google charges your account; Tramo never sees or stores your payment details. RevenueCat is not used on Android.
  • Community reports: in this Android version, reports you create stay on your phone and are not shared with other drivers. If they are shared in the future, this policy will be updated first.
  • Android permissions: precise and approximate location (to guide you and warn you about speed cameras); location foreground service (to keep guiding you with the screen off, you will see a persistent notification); notifications and exact alarms (only if you schedule a departure time). You can change them anytime in Android Settings.
  • Android Auto: if you connect it, Tramo shows the map and directions on the car screen; the data is still the phone’s.
  • The other services listed below (DGT, OpenStreetMap, OpenFreeMap, fuel prices and traffic cameras) are used just as on iPhone.

Who we are

Tramo is a navigation app developed by Iván Sevilla. For any privacy question, contact us from the support page.

External services the app queries

To show what’s on your way, Tramo requests public data from these services sending only an area or a route, never your name or an identifier:

  • Apple Maps: routes and search.
  • Spain’s traffic authority (DGT): incidents, speed cameras, traffic cameras and message signs.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass): speed limits, lanes, red light cameras and schools. Speed cameras worldwide (OpenStreetMap plus official data from Norway, Bogotá and Buenos Aires) are already built into the app: nothing is sent for them.
  • OpenFreeMap: the 3D world map.
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit): weather.
  • adsb.lol: nearby helicopters (public aviation data).
  • Spanish and French government open data: fuel prices and chargers.

Traffic cameras (Tramo Pro): images come from public bodies (DGT and Madrid City Council in Spain; Caltrans, Transport for London, DriveBC, City of Toronto, Fintraffic, Luxembourg’s CITA, Hong Kong’s Transport Department and Singapore’s LTA). The app downloads the full camera list and picks the ones near you on your iPhone: your location is not sent to these sources, only the photos you view are requested.

Each has its own privacy policy; like any internet service, they can see the IP address a request comes from.

Subscription provider: RevenueCat

RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) checks Tramo Pro purchases on our behalf and gives us aggregated subscription statistics. It acts as a data processor: it only handles the app’s anonymous identifier, your Tramo Pro purchase history, iOS and app version, language and store country, and the request’s IP address. It is not used for advertising or tracking. Transfers to the USA rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. More: revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Tramo does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads and no advertising or behavioural analytics SDKs. The only third-party SDK is RevenueCat, for subscriptions.
  • No tracking across apps or websites (no advertising identifier).
  • No selling of data.

Permissions

Location: “While Using the App”. With a route in progress, iOS lets Tramo keep guiding you with the screen off or from CarPlay (you’ll see the blue indicator). Notifications: only if you schedule a departure time. You can change permissions anytime in the iPhone’s Settings.

Your rights

As your data lives on your iPhone, you can delete it anytime by deleting the app. Community reports you send expire shortly; for anything else (access, rectification or erasure under the GDPR), contact us from the support page. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Tramo is an app for drivers and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
